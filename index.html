Research

My PhD asked why mitochondria fail in sepsis-induced acute lung injury, and whether protecting them reduces lung damage. Using LPS-stimulated macrophages and lung epithelial cells, together with caecal ligation and puncture and intratracheal LPS mouse models, I found that PINK1/Parkin mitophagy is impaired in the injured lung. Restoring it with urolithin A reduced mitochondrial dysfunction, inflammasome activation and lung inflammation, and shifted macrophage polarisation (Mitochondrion, 2025).

I now work on an ICMR-funded project on long non-coding RNAs in lung development and sepsis-induced lung injury in neonatal mice.

Where I want to go next

  • Mitochondrial quality control upstream of mitophagyHow single mitochondrial proteins are checked, repaired or degraded, and how failure of these systems is signalled.
  • Mitochondria as triggers of innate immunitymtDNA release, cGAS-STING and inflammasome signalling in macrophages.
  • Lung inflammation and repairHow immunometabolic changes in macrophages shape epithelial injury and recovery.
